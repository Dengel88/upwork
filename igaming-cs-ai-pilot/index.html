SECTION 01

First Steps: What We Investigate & Change First (and Why)

We do not start by putting an unconstrained chatbot in front of players. In iGaming, players messaging about money are anxious; generic bot replies destroy trust. Instead, our first 72 hours focus on eliminating the internal CS-to-Payments ping-pong:

1. Audit 500 Recent Deposit Transcripts

Classify deposit enquiries by root cause across your target market's payment rails (cards, local bank transfer/QR, crypto, e-wallets). Typically, 55–65% don't require external PSP contact at all—the status is already visible in backoffice/webhook logs (`Pending SLA`, `User Declined`, `Wrong Deposit Rail`, `Unclaimed Bonus`).

2. Standardize Intake & Slip OCR First

Change the very first interaction in LiveChat: before a human CS agent ever touches a deposit ticket, the AI extracts the Player UID, performs Vision OCR on the payment receipt/slip (`Amount`, `Timestamp`, `UTR/Reference`, `Sender Name`), and cross-checks it against the Backoffice Deposit Table.

3. Decouple Status Lookups from Payments Team

Give the AI orchestrator (and CS) automated read-only lookup access to deposit transaction logs and PSP webhook callbacks so CS never has to message the Payments Team just to ask "What is the status of TxID #99412?"

SECTION 03

Tools, Models, Data & Integrations Needed (and Why)

Architecture & Model Stack

1. Channel Integration: LiveChat.com Agent Bot API & Webhooks (supports customer-facing replies, canned buttons, file/image attachments, tags, and internal Whisper notes for seamless agent handover). Modular adapter ready for LINE Messaging API later.

2. Orchestration & Guardrails: Python (FastAPI) + n8n workflow engine. Why? Deposit handling must follow strict deterministic state rules (never hallucinate transaction states or promise refunds) while n8n gives your ops team visual visibility into every PSP/Payments webhook.

3. LLM & Vision OCR Models: Gemini 2.5 Pro / Flash or Claude 4.5 Sonnet with structured JSON schema outputs. Chosen for industry-leading multilingual accuracy (EN, TH, VI, ID, JA, ZH, PT/ES) and high-precision Vision OCR on mobile banking slips & crypto receipts.

Data & Access Required (Strict Least-Privilege)

1. Historical Chat Sample (Read-Only): 300–500 anonymized LiveChat transcripts covering Deposit, Withdrawal, and Game-Loss tickets + current CS SOPs/macros.

2. Backoffice / PAM Read-Only Endpoint: A single read-only API endpoint (or replica view) taking player_id and returning: Last 5 deposit attempts (status, amount, rail, PSP code, timestamp), KYC state, VIP tier, and active wagering requirement.

3. Payments / PSP Escalation Channel: Webhook to your Payments Team ticketing tool (Jira, Slack, Telegram, or internal Backoffice queue) to push pre-validated deposit investigation packets directly without manual CS re-typing.

SECTION 04

Limitations, Risks & Assumptions That Must Be Validated

1. External PSP API Granularity (Key Assumption)

Some local iGaming payment providers (especially regional bank-transfer aggregators) do not expose real-time query APIs for missing deposits and rely on Skype/Telegram/Slack groups with the PSP. Mitigation: Even when a PSP lacks a status API, the AI still eliminates 70% of CS work by OCR-validating the receipt, checking the internal ledger, and auto-formatting the exact PSP inquiry message for 1-click dispatch.

2. Fake / Doctored Payment Screenshots

In iGaming, players frequently submit edited bank slips or duplicate receipts from old transactions. Mitigation: The AI never credits balances or promises credit based on an image alone—it checks image metadata, compares the extracted UTR/Reference ID against historical used references, and flags anomalies for Payments review.

3. Unauthenticated Players in LiveChat

If LiveChat is launched before login (or without passing signed session variables), the AI cannot safely disclose account-specific financial details. Assumption to validate: LiveChat widget passes authenticated user_id / session token, or we enforce a 1-step OTP/UID verification flow first.

4. Player Frustration & VIP Sensitivity

High-value VIPs or players experiencing perceived "game-loss" bugs will churn if trapped in an AI loop. Mitigation: Hard deterministic bypass: VIP tiers, keywords like "rigged/scam/lawyer", or 2 consecutive unresolved turns trigger an instant (<2s) human handover.
