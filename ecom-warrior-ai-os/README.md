## What Is Included in This Prototype

1. **`index.html` — Interactive 3-Tab Command Center:**
   - **Tab 1: AI Sales Call Intelligence & Closer Coach** — Paste or load high-ticket enrollment call transcripts to compute Discovery Depth, Objection Isolation (Price, Spouse/Partner, Time/Skepticism), Closer Talk-to-Listen Ratio, and auto-generate GoHighLevel (GHL) follow-up SMS/Email drafts + webhook payloads.
   - **Tab 2: Executive $10M+ AUD Revenue & Bottleneck Radar** — Live visibility into Cash Collected, Setter-to-Closer Show Rate, Closer Leaderboard & Objection Leakage Matrix, and autonomous Slack anomaly alerts.
   - **Tab 3: First 30-Day Claude Code + MCP Roadmap & ROI Simulator** — Week-by-week architecture plan and interactive AUD revenue lift calculator.
2. **`sales_call_auditor.py` & `test_auditor.py`:**
   - Deterministic + heuristic Python engine for parsing speaker turns, scoring closer rubrics, detecting unresolved objections and missed buying signals, and emitting GoHighLevel-compatible webhook payloads.
3. **`PROPOSAL.md`:**
   - Complete answers to Ecom Warrior Academy's screening questions and Fixed-Price Milestone rollout plan.
