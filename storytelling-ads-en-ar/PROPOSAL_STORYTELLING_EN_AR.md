### 2. How I Maintain Character & Visual Consistency Across 35–45 Shots (Up to 3 Minutes)
Pure text-to-video falls apart after 5 seconds. As demonstrated in the interactive studio link above (**Mode A**), my production pipeline locks identity across full 3-minute narrative arcs:
1. **Multi-Angle Character Turnaround Bible (`Nano Banana Pro` / `Flux LoRA`):** Before generating a single frame of video, I build a 6-angle 4K reference sheet for each protagonist (Front, 3/4 Left, 3/4 Right, Profile, Warm Indoor Key, Night Rim-Light) with locked wardrobe and color LUTs.
2. **Start/End Keyframe Pre-Visualization:** I generate exact start and end keyframes for all 35–45 shots so camera framing, eye-lines, and props match across cuts.
3. **Motion & Lip-Sync Generation (`Kling 3.0 Pro` Elements + `Seedance 2.5` + `ElevenLabs Studio`):** I drive motion from the locked keyframes and synchronize dialogue close-ups to native English and Arabic voice stems within ±0.2s syllable accuracy.
4. **Cinematic Finishing (`Premiere Pro` / `DaVinci Resolve`):** Film grain unification, 24fps optical-flow smoothing, layered foley/music stems, and broadcast-safe EN + RTL Arabic subtitles.
